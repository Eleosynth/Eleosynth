Eleosynth for Windows
=====================
Run the Setup .exe. It installs for your account only (no administrator password).
The installer isn't signed with a paid certificate yet, so Windows SmartScreen may say it
"protected your PC": choose More info, then Run anyway.

The portable zip needs no install: unzip it and open Eleosynth.exe.
Plug your guitar into an audio interface, open the Pedalboard tab and press Connect guitar input.
