Eleosynth for Mac (Apple Silicon)
=================================
1. Drag Eleosynth into the Applications folder.
2. The first time, macOS may say it can't check the app for malicious software, because it isn't
   notarised with a paid Apple certificate yet. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down,
   and choose "Open Anyway" next to Eleosynth. You only do this once.
3. When you connect a guitar or microphone, allow microphone access.

Plug your guitar into an audio interface, open the Pedalboard tab and press "Connect guitar input".
Use headphones while monitoring to avoid feedback.
